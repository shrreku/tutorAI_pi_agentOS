Consent & data use

A plain-language summary of what TutorBook processes, where it lives, and how the model uses your study materials. Review and set your preferences before continuing.

What data is processed

  • Sources you upload (PDFs, slides, notes) for Organic Chemistry I and other notebooks.
  • Your learning state — quiz results, flashcard ratings, and mastery per concept.
  • Tutor conversations and the artifacts generated from them.
  • Basic account details: name, email, and tutor-credit balance.

Source storage

Your 7 ingested sources (about 1,200 pages) are stored encrypted at rest in your private workspace.

  • Sources are scoped to your account and never shared between learners.
  • Indexed embeddings power retrieval and citations like Ch. 7 · p. 142.
  • Delete a source at any time and its index is purged within 24 hours.

Model usage

No training on your data

Your sources, conversations, and learning state are never used to train foundation models. They are used only to power your tutoring sessions and generate your study artifacts.

Model responses are grounded in your uploaded sources with inline citations so you can verify every claim against the original material.
