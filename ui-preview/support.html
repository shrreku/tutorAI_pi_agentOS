How do I add a source to my notebook?

Open a notebook such as Organic Chemistry I, head to Sources, and upload a PDF, slide deck, or paste a link. TutorBook ingests it (Ch.7 took ~0.3 tutor-hours) and adds it to the 7 sources already in the notebook.

What are tutor credits and when do they reset?

Credits meter tutoring time and artifact generation. You currently have 71% remaining (~18 tutor-hours), resetting on 1 July. You can top up at any time and the balance rolls into the current cycle.

How does the tutor decide what I need to review?

The tutor scores each concept on your study map. For example, stereochemistry is at 45% and flagged as “needs review”, so it appears higher in suggested next steps and live plans.

Can I see the evidence behind an answer?

Yes. Any tutor response with a citation has an Evidence button that opens the source passage in a drawer, so you can verify claims against your own materials.

How do I change my theme or account settings?

Switch themes from the floating nav at the bottom of any page (6 themes available). Profile, notifications, and billing live in Settings and Account.
