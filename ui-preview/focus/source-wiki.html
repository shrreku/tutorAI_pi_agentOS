Nucleophilic substitution

Nucleophilic substitution is a fundamental class of reactions in organic chemistry in which an electron-rich nucleophile displaces a leaving group from an electrophilic carbon.

Definition

The two main mechanisms are SN1 (unimolecular, via carbocation) and SN2 (bimolecular, concerted backside attack).

Rate formula

Rate_SN2 = k[RX][Nu⁻] — bimolecular, second order.
