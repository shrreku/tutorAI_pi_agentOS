Page 142 · 7.3 The SN2 Mechanism

The SN2 mechanism proceeds through a single concerted transition state in which the nucleophile approaches the substrate carbon from the side opposite the leaving group.

Highlighted · cited in tutor

Primary substrates react fastest because steric hindrance is minimized at the transition state.
