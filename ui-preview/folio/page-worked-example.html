Predicting the SN2 product

Step-reveal worked solution · Ch. 7

Problem

What is the major product when (S)-2-bromobutane reacts with OH⁻ via an SN2 pathway?

STEP 1 · Identify the substrate

(S)-2-bromobutane is a secondary alkyl halide — SN2 is feasible.

STEP 2 · Backside attack

OH⁻ attacks the chiral carbon from the side opposite the C–Br bond.

STEP 3 · Stereochemical inversion

Walden inversion gives (R)-2-butanol as the major product.

Answer

(R)-2-butanol, with inversion of configuration at C2.
